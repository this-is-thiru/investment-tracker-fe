Performance Calculations

Determine how system return rates and growth percentages are calculated

TWR (Time-Weighted)

Measures the compound growth rate of the portfolio. Recommended for tracking portfolio manager performance because it eliminates cash inflow/outflow distortions.

Recommended
IRR (Money-Weighted)

Computes the internal rate of return. Accounts for the timing and size of cash transactions. Best for reflecting your personal dollar-earned returns.

Simple Return

Basic gain percentage calculation: (Current Value - Total Invested) / Total Invested. Simple to understand, but does not account for holding duration.
